# Late Morning — Episode 1

When I woke up, the world was gone.

The sun was halfway across my bed. I grabbed my phone. Ten seventeen. Wednesday. I was already two hours late for work.

I knocked my glass off the nightstand. It hit the rug. I could hear the water soaking in. That's how quiet it was.

I went to the window. At the intersection, the light went green. A white car just sat there. Its driver's door was open.

Across the hall, Martin's door was open too. I called his name. His radio was playing in the kitchen. I waited for the song to end. Another one started.

Then I heard a dog bark. I knew him from the bakery. I went downstairs and untied him. He pulled me toward the car, then stopped short of the open door.

That's when I heard the alarm. A little red clock was going off in the back seat. It said 10:17, the same time I'd seen when I woke up.
