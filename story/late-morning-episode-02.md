# Late Morning — Episode 2: The Lights Went Out

**Format:** First-person 9:16 Reel, about 90 seconds. Daniel Ward narrates from Day 18. The highway and gas station are part of his account of Day 1.

## 0:00–0:12 · Day 18

**Picture:** An unfamiliar bedroom at **7:12 AM**. Daniel's boots are still on. The dog lifts his head from the floor by the door. On screen: **DAY 18**.

**Daniel:** It's been eighteen days since everyone disappeared. This morning I woke up in somebody else's bed. I don't even know whose. But it started with that alarm.

## 0:12–0:25 · Day 1

**Picture:** Match cut to the red **10:17** alarm from Episode 1. On screen: **DAY 1**. Daniel goes back for Martin's portable radio. It plays as he lifts it. Cut to **3:17 PM**. The refrigerator stops and the house goes dark. Across the street, a traffic light dies.

**Daniel:** I went back for Martin's radio. It was still playing when I picked it up. Five hours after I'd first woken up, the power went out.

## 0:25–0:40 · The plant

**Picture:** Through the window, the plant rises beyond the rooftops. Cut to the paper atlas. Daniel's hometown and the plant are nearly under the same thumb.

**Daniel:** The plant was just outside town. Even if the reactor shut itself down, it still needed cooling. The backup diesels could buy time. If they quit and the cooling went with them, the core could melt.

## 0:40–0:56 · West

**Picture:** Keys off the table. Two water jugs and four cans go into his father's pickup. The dog walks to the open passenger door. Cut to him safely on the seat. Martin's radio rides in the cab. The pickup passes parked cars and goes beneath the **WEST / ROUTE 6** sign.

**Daniel:** I took Dad's truck. Two jugs of water, four cans, the radio, and the dog. I drove west until I couldn't see the plant in the mirrors.

## 0:56–1:12 · Last Stop Fuel

**Picture:** The fuel needle sits above empty. Daniel turns in past the mechanical price sign. The pump screen is blank. He squeezes the handle and gets nothing. The dog waits in the cab as Daniel heads toward the shop.

**Daniel:** The first gas station still had its prices up. I pulled in anyway. Nothing came out of the pump. I went inside to see what else I could take.

## 1:12–1:30 · Under the counter

**Picture:** The door bell jingles. Daniel sweeps past dark coolers and sparse shelves. Two sealed bottles and an atlas sit behind the register. The note on top says **IF YOU'RE GOING WEST, USE THE OLD COUNTY ROAD. I LEFT THIS AT NOON.** Daniel looks back toward the pickup. The dog is looking past him, into the shop. Cut to black.

**Daniel:** Somebody had left water and a map. The old county road was marked in red. I read the last line twice. Noon. While I was still in town thinking I was the only one left.
