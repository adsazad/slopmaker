# Late Morning series

## Opening line

Start every video with Daniel saying, “It was the [day] since everyone disappeared.” Use the day shown in the episode, even when Daniel is narrating from a later day. For Day 2, the first spoken line is “It was the second day since everyone disappeared.”

Put the line first in the narration script and at the start of the exported video so viewers immediately know what happened and where they are in the timeline.
