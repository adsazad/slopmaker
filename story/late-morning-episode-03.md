# Late Morning — Episode 3: The Pumps Were Dead

**Format:** First-person vertical Reel, 75 seconds. Daniel narrates from Day 18 about the second day after everyone disappeared.

## Story

It was the second day since everyone disappeared. I'd been driving for fifteen hours.

Dad used to keep a little fuel in a can in the truck. I ran through that too. I kept driving until I found a gas station and pulled in beside the pumps. There were other cars and trucks parked there already. Nobody inside them.

I was too scared to get out. Every mile made the road feel less empty, and every time I stopped, I was sure I would see someone waiting outside the truck. So I climbed into the back seat and tried to sleep with the doors locked.

When morning came, I finally stepped out. The pumps were dead. I could see the big storage tanks behind the station. There had to be fuel in them. But I had no tools to get it out.

I was hungry, so I took what food I could find inside the station. Bags of chips, a few other snacks, and cans. I don't know how to cook, so the cans stayed shut. I'd been surviving on chips and whatever else I could eat straight from the wrapper.

I searched around the station and found a length of pipe. The other cars were still there. I used a rock to open a few fuel inlets and siphoned what I could. I filled two containers and the truck's tank. Maybe enough for another day.

There was no sign of anyone. No voices, no movement, no clue where everyone had gone. Every town I'd passed was empty. Beside the station, a police van sat with its doors unlocked. I took the radio from it and switched it on. Just shhh.

Martin’s radio was still hissing, same as it had been since the power went out. The police radio did the same. I got back in and started driving toward my aunt's house, hoping someone would be there.
