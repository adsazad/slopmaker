# LINE 13
## Episode 1: “14 Old Bell Road”

**Format:** vertical 9:16 horror short, about 75 seconds  
**Tone:** grounded small-town unease that turns into supernatural doppelgänger horror  
**Hook:** On his first night shift, a grid technician gets a blackout call from a street that is not on any map.

## Story

I moved to Morrow Creek for a job that was supposed to be boring. The power company needed a night technician, and I needed a fresh start.

The town had one diner, one water tower, and windows that went dark before ten.

At 1:13 a.m. on my first shift, the outage board flashed. One customer was drawing power from Line 13.

**14 Old Bell Road.**

Dispatch said, “That road isn’t in our system.”

My truck GPS showed a gray line leading past the last streetlight. At the end stood a house with one upstairs window lit. The meter outside was turning backwards.

I stepped out to check the service number. Dispatch broke in: “Eli, get back in the truck.”

Then my own voice came through the radio, breathless and terrified.

“Don’t shut it off. It’s already inside.”

The upstairs curtain moved. A little girl pressed three fingers to the glass.

Behind her stood a man in my work jacket, holding my radio.

He leaned close to the window.

“You came early this time.”

Every light in the house went out.

The outage board refreshed. A second customer had connected to Line 13.

My new address.

I hadn’t told anyone where I was staying.

## Scene breakdown

Keep the first half quiet and observational. Let the viewer notice the wrong details
before Eli does. Save the sharpest sound and fastest cut for the double in the window.

### Scene 1 — The hook (0:00–0:05)

- **Visual:** Extreme close-up of a utility outage map. A red feeder line pulses; a new address types itself onto the screen: `14 OLD BELL ROAD`.
- **Camera:** Vertical macro shot, slight handheld drift toward the address.
- **Voice-over:** “My first night on the power grid, a house that wasn’t on the map called in a blackout.”
- **On-screen text:** `THE ADDRESS DIDN’T EXIST.`
- **Sound:** Low transformer hum. One dry radio click as the address appears.

### Scene 2 — New town, night shift (0:05–0:13)

- **Visual:** Eli’s utility truck passes Morrow Creek’s single stoplight, a dark diner, and the water tower. His transfer paperwork sits on the passenger seat; it shows his rental address as `12 Foundry Lane`.
- **Camera:** Windshield view, then a brief close-up of Eli reflected in the glass.
- **Voice-over:** “I’d just transferred to Morrow Creek. The town went to bed early. The grid didn’t.”
- **Sound:** Tires on wet pavement; sparse crickets; the electrical hum stays under the scene.

### Scene 3 — The rule (0:13–0:21)

- **Visual:** At the small control room, supervisor Cole circles `LINE 13` on a faded grid map. The rest of the board looks ordinary.
- **Camera:** Over Cole’s shoulder, then close on his finger covering a section of the map.
- **Dialogue — Cole:** “If Line 13 wakes up by itself, don’t go looking for the fault. Call me.”
- **Eli:** “Why?”
- **Cole:** “Because there’s nothing out there.”
- **Sound:** Fluorescent light buzz; the marker squeaks across the paper.

### Scene 4 — The call (0:21–0:30)

- **Visual:** Digital clock flips to `1:13 A.M.` The board flashes one new load and prints the Old Bell Road ticket. Eli keys the radio.
- **Camera:** Clock → warning light reflected in Eli’s eye → ticket in his hand.
- **Dialogue — Eli:** “Dispatch, can you locate fourteen Old Bell?”
- **Dialogue — Mae (radio):** “Negative. There is no Old Bell Road.”
- **Sound:** Alarm tone cuts off too quickly; the room falls nearly silent.

### Scene 5 — Past the last streetlight (0:30–0:41)

- **Visual:** Eli drives beyond the final lit street. The GPS draws a thin gray road through empty space. A single upstairs window glows ahead.
- **Camera:** Wide windshield view, then low angle outside the truck as it slows beside a utility pole marked `13`.
- **Voice-over:** “My GPS still had a route. It just didn’t have a road.”
- **Sound:** Engine idle; radio static briefly forms a whisper, then clears.

### Scene 6 — The backward meter (0:41–0:51)

- **Visual:** Eli stays beside the truck and reads the service tag from a distance. The meter wheel spins backward. The house looks old but newly lit.
- **Camera:** Slow push toward the reversed meter; rack focus to the upstairs curtain.
- **Dialogue — Mae (radio):** “Eli, get back in the truck. Now.”
- **Sound:** Meter ticking backward, growing louder than it should. No music.

### Scene 7 — The voice on Line 13 (0:51–1:05)

- **Visual:** Three fingers press against the upstairs glass. In the room behind the girl, a second Eli appears in the same work jacket, holding a radio. The real Eli is visible in the truck mirror, frozen outside.
- **Camera:** Three slow taps in close-up; reveal the girl; a final focus shift finds the double behind her.
- **Radio — Eli’s voice:** “Don’t shut it off. It’s already inside.”
- **Sound:** Three clear taps. On the last word, cut the low hum completely.

### Scene 8 — The new customer (1:05–1:15)

- **Visual:** The double leans toward the window and smiles. Cut to black as every light in the house snaps off. The control-room map reappears; a second load lights up at `12 FOUNDRY LANE`.
- **Dialogue — Double:** “You came early this time.”
- **Voice-over:** “A second customer had connected to Line 13. My new address.”
- **On-screen text:** `LINE 13 — PART 1`  
  Optional last beat: `I NEVER GAVE THEM MY ADDRESS.`
- **Sound:** One heavy power-down thump, then a phone vibration in the darkness.

## Series thread to carry forward

Do not explain Line 13 in the pilot. Keep the old road, the backwards meter, and
the phrase “this time” as clues. The larger mystery is that Morrow Creek’s grid
still registers c ustomers and streets the town has erased. Each later episode
can reveal one more impossible address while bringing a different roster
character into Eli’s investigation.

## Scene assets to make next

- Eli Mercer, the night grid technician; his double can reuse the same model.
- Utility truck cab, radio handset, and a readable outage-map screen.
- Morrow Creek street, final streetlight, line pole marked `13`, and the isolated house.
- Reversible-looking meter prop and one warm upstairs window.

The existing character GLBs are kept out of the pilot’s mystery reveal. We can
introduce them as Eli’s allies in later scenes once the story gives each one a
reason to enter.
