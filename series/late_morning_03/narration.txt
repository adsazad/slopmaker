It was the second day since everyone disappeared. I'd been driving for fifteen hours. Dad kept a little fuel in the truck. I'd used that too.

I pulled up at a gas station and locked the doors. The longer I drove, the more scared I was to get out. I took a nap in the back seat and waited for morning.

The pumps were dead. I knew there was fuel in the big tanks. I had no tools to get it out.

Inside, I found chips and cans. I don't know how to cook, so I'd been living on whatever came in a bag.

I found a pipe and took fuel from a few parked cars. I filled two containers and the truck. Maybe enough for another day.

I took a radio from a police van. Just static. Martin's radio had been doing that since the power went out.

Every town was empty. I started driving again. I was going to my aunt's house. I had to see if she was there.
