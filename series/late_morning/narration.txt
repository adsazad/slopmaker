When I woke up, the world was gone. The buildings were still there. So were the cars and the traffic lights. It was the people who were missing.

I didn’t know that yet. I just knew I was late for work.

The sun was halfway across my bed when I opened my eyes. Usually, it was still on the wall by the time I left. I reached for my phone and squinted at the screen.

Ten seventeen.

I hadn’t slept past seven on a weekday in years. I checked the day, hoping I’d got it wrong. Wednesday.

I sat up so fast I knocked my water glass off the nightstand. It hit the rug and rolled under the bed. I was halfway down to get it when I realized I could hear the water trickling out.

That was how quiet it was.

I went to the window. A white car was stopped at the light. The light went green, and the car stayed where it was.

Then I noticed the driver’s door was open.
