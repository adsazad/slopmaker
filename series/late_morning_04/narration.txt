It was the third day since everyone disappeared. I reached my aunt's street just before dark. Cars were parked all over it. I had to weave between them.

Her front door was unlocked. The spare key was still under the pot. I called out and went room to room. There was nobody. I took my cousin's gamepad. I don't know why.

I found Uncle's socket set, pliers, a tire gauge, and a foot pump. I checked the soft rear tire and pumped it up. Then I slept in the truck. I couldn't bring myself to sleep in the house.

In the morning, birds covered the power lines. When they took off, there were more behind them. All flying west. If the birds were coming into town, what else would follow? For the first time, I thought I might need a gun.

Then I saw the smoke. It was coming from the direction of the plant. The wind was carrying it east. I didn't know what had happened out there. I just knew I wanted more distance.

I put the spare key back under the pot. I couldn't stop thinking about that note on the map. I'd driven all this way. No sign of whoever left it. Every gas station looked untouched. I drove west again.
